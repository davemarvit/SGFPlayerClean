SGF PLAYER — OFFLINE REVIEW

TO LAUNCH
In Finder, press Command-Shift-G, enter ~/Applications, and press Return.
Double-click "SGF Player Offline Review.app" there.
You do not need Xcode or Terminal. Look for the orange OFFLINE REVIEW banner.
Your working SGFPlayerClean app remains separate.

The app now lives outside Documents so loading its bundled examples and engine
resources does not require Documents access. Use this Applications copy rather
than any older copy in the review folder. Opening your own protected folders
may still require permission. Reports and exported Sample Games remain here.

This app has fresh, separate settings and AI caches. OGS connections are
blocked by macOS, and there is no Online tab. Wi-Fi can stay on.
The existing KataGo installation is used locally; give it a moment to load.

A QUICK FIRST TEST
1. The bundled ko example opens paused. Advance one move using the playback
   controls. Black captures one white stone: the capture count and lid should
   both show one, and the empty B8 point should have a soft red marker.
2. Try to put White back at B8. The app should refuse immediate recapture.
3. Step backward: that capture should disappear from the count and lid.
4. Switch between 2D and 3D. Rotate the 3D board and watch the lid stone.
5. Open the right sidebar using the panel icon at the upper right, then choose
   AI. Analysis is enabled only on the AI tab; opening another SGF selects Local.
   Candidate moves and the analysis graph should appear for the displayed position.

MOVE SLIDER
While dragging, the board and capture lids preview the position together at a
limit of five updates per second. Release applies the exact selected move. Preview moves are
silent and new AI requests wait until release. Your source SGF is never rewritten.

The on-screen back/forward arrows move one move per click. Hold Shift while
clicking to move ten moves, just like Shift + Left/Right Arrow on the keyboard.
This works in both 2D and 3D.

LOCAL FINAL SCORING
Local play now has a Pass button beside the playback controls in 2D and 3D.
Pause playback and go to the last recorded move to enable it. Passing changes
the turn without adding or removing stones. You can rewind and replay passes.
After two passes, the scoring sidebar opens automatically.

1. Select "05 Japanese scoring" in the Local list (or open it from Sample Games).
2. Click Pass twice. The sidebar changes to Review score.
3. Click either of the two white stones at the upper-left corner (A9 or A8).
   Both should fade. Soft black and white dots show each player's territory;
   empty points without dots are neutral.
4. Check Black 24, White 33.5, then click Confirm score.
   Final Score should say "Alice [White] wins by 9.5 points".
5. Edit scoring lets you change the marks; Resume play restores the board for
   normal moves. Rewinding hides scoring marks; returning to the end restores
   the review, including a confirmed result.

Clicking any stone toggles its connected group. Neutral areas mode lets you
exclude an empty region from territory (click again to restore it), including
eyes in Japanese/Korean seki. Life and death are reviewed by you, not decided
automatically. Verify rules and komi before confirming; missing/unsupported
rules or missing/invalid komi require an explicit choice.

"06 New Zealand scoring" uses the same position with area scoring and 7 komi.
After marking the same white group, Black has 36 and White 43: Alice wins by 7.
Captured stones and marked-dead stones are not added again under area scoring.
The lids keep actual captures; scoring-only dead stones appear in the score
breakdown. Marks/results stay in memory for this review session; SGFs are not
rewritten. Changing games or quitting clears the local review.

SCORING DISPLAY EXAMPLES
Click "Score examples…" at the right of the orange banner.
The first example should say "Final Score" beside "Alice [White] wins by
6.5 points", with totals Black 29 and White 35.5.
Use the Example menu to check a Black win, draw, fractional score, and a
game with no numeric score. Edit the player names to try long or empty names.
The earlier-position toggle shows the notice used when reviewing past moves
after a game has finished. Click Done (or Escape) to return to your board.
These are illustrative display examples; they do not score or change your game.

PLAYBACK SPEED AND SOUND
Settings > Move Delay controls the time between moves (smaller is faster).
Changes apply while playing; changing it while paused does not start playback.
Settings > Stone Volume controls both placement and capture audio.

MORE EXAMPLES
Select examples in the Local list. They stay paused until you press Play.
The two self-capture examples deliberately start with the same stones and have
no recorded moves to play back. To see the difference, manually click B9 as
Black: the empty second intersection from the left in the top row. This fills
the black group's last liberty (its last adjacent empty point).
- Two stone handicap: starts with two black stones and White to move.
- Japanese self capture: try Black at B9 (second point in the top row).
  The move should be rejected.
- New Zealand self capture: the same move is permitted; two black stones
  disappear and White's captured count increases by two.

YOUR OWN SGF FILE
Use File > Open SGF File... (Command-O). Opening a file reads it; it does not
rewrite it. You can use a copy if you prefer. The menu loads it paused.

The review app does not yet simulate the OGS end-game scoring exchange.
Local territory remains an estimate; live scoring acceptance and final-score
synchronization are still reserved for a later OGS test.

IF SOMETHING LOOKS WRONG
Note which example or file, the move number, 2D or 3D, and what you saw.
A screenshot helps, but no repeated test sequence is required.
Quit this app with Command-Q when finished; launch your usual app normally.
