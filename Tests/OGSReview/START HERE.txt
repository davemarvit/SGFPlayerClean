SGF PLAYER — OGS REVIEW

In Finder, press Command-Shift-G, enter ~/Applications, and press Return.
Open "SGF Player OGS Review.app". Look for the blue OGS REVIEW banner.
The working app and Offline Review app remain separate.

FOUR MODES
Play Online: your challenge lobby, with the existing board size, speed, rated,
  and eligibility filters. Create or accept a challenge here. Lobby returns
  to the list without resigning; Return to Game reopens your game.
Local Play: place moves for both sides. New Game starts a fresh board.
  Pass and playback controls are at the bottom. Review this game makes a
  separate review copy.
Observe: choose a live game, or enter its OGS link/ID. Browse games opens the
  popup; Cancel or Escape closes it. Games returns to the list.
  Games are ordered by the stronger player, with ranks, move count and time
  control. Scroll to load more; Refresh updates the list and move counts.
Review: load saved SGFs and use playback. To add moves, choose Continue this
  position in Local Play. Local Play and Review preserve separate positions
  while switching modes within this session.

AI analysis is a separate toggle. It works in Local Play, Review, and Observe
for other players' games. It is unavailable in Play Online and while your
signed-in account has a known ongoing online game, even if you change modes.
Observe is read-only. Play Online now permits actual game/challenge actions.

Your username and Log Out are at the top. Sign in from Play Online or Observe
if needed. Login persists across restarts unless you log out or OGS expires
it. Changing modes does not sign you out or close the OGS connection.

UPDATED GAME CONTROLS
Pass and Resign are next to each other. Resign asks before sending anything.
If you rewind an online game, Return to latest move restores the live position.
A dropped connection keeps the game panel open and offers Reconnect.
Undo requests can be declined or cancelled. Failed SGF downloads show an error.

Only session cookies are saved, in this app's private data folder; no password
is saved. No working-app credentials, settings, or library are imported.
Unsaved local games are not automatically exported on quit.
Network access is enabled here; the installed Offline Review app remains blocked.
