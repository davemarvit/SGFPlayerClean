SGF PLAYER — OGS REVIEW

In Finder, press Command-Shift-G, enter ~/Applications, and press Return.
Open "SGF Player OGS Review.app". Look for the blue OGS REVIEW banner.
The working app and Offline Review app remain separate.

FIRST TEST: LOGIN AND OBSERVE
1. Click Log In in the Online sidebar. Enter your OGS credentials in the app.
2. Wait for "Ready to observe" and your username.
3. In your browser, choose a public game on OGS and copy its game link.
4. Paste the link (or its numeric game ID) into this app and click Observe Game.
5. Check that the players, board, and latest move match the browser. For a live
   game, wait for a new move and check that it arrives without reopening it.
6. Try 2D and 3D. To choose another game, click Observe game… in the blue banner.

This build cannot submit moves, passes, resignations, undo, scoring agreement,
challenges, automatch requests, or chat messages. It can read game chat.
Even your own games are treated as observation only. KataGo stays disabled
while observing a live game.

Login cookies are kept in memory for this app session. Quitting requires a new
login next time. No working-app credentials, settings, or library are imported.
Network access is enabled in this app; the Offline Review app remains blocked.

If login or observation fails, report the visible error and which step failed.
Do not send your password, cookies, or authentication tokens.
