# Finished-game controls, mistake filters, accuracy, and capture piles

The finished-game result is an inline banner in the scrolling game information.
Save SGF, Review game, and Back to lobby/games occupy a separate footer. While
playing, Pass and Resign remain together in that footer. The result never floats
over export controls, even at short window heights.

AI review exposes Both/Black/White and an inclusive minimum point-loss filter,
with slider, numerical entry, matching count, and analysed-move coverage for the
selected line. The graph highlights the same matching moves as the red arrows.
The arrows now navigate to turn + 1 (the position after the evaluated move),
correcting the old zero-based indexing error. At either end they disable rather
than jumping to the beginning/end. Missing evaluations are not counted. The
existing quality categories and recent-move rings retain their established
calibration; filtering does not change overall performance statistics.

The quality table includes mean nonnegative point loss and separate Black/White
accuracy. Accuracy independently implements KaTrain's game_report formula:
https://github.com/sanderland/katrain/blob/master/katrain/core/ai.py
https://github.com/sanderland/katrain/blob/master/katrain/core/game_node.py
https://github.com/sanderland/katrain/blob/master/katrain/core/constants.py

For each move, candidate prior-weighted nonnegative score loss estimates position
difficulty, capped at 1. Weight = clamp(max(difficulty, played loss / 4), .05, 1).
Accuracy = 100 * .75 ^ (sum(weight * played loss) / sum(weight)). All comparisons
use KataGo's configured side-to-move perspective, including consecutive handicap
placements. This is a game-performance score, not a rank or winning probability.
Optional prior/order fields are now retained by the existing Codable cache.
Legacy caches remain readable and supply mean loss; accuracy is unavailable when
any evaluated move lacks valid candidate priors. Newly computed analysis includes
those fields. The app does not discard or automatically recompute old caches just
to populate accuracy. Coverage indicates partial analysis; filters do not affect
which moves contribute to the statistics.

2D lids replace the count-dependent golden-angle spiral with a cached seeded
settling approximation. Random landing candidates compete on supported height,
with a small random energy term, varied tilt, and orientation. Positions depend
on capture sequence and seed, not final count. Rewinds reuse prefixes and later
captures do not reshuffle earlier ones. Per-session game identities and distinct
Black/White seed salts vary the piles between games and lids. Snapshot restoration
preserves the seed. No physics runs continuously for 2D.

3D retains SceneKit physics with rounded flattened convex hulls replacing cylinder
collision shapes. Moderate friction/angular damping lets stones settle at varied
angles. Rotated/offset nonintersecting drop batches and independently seeded stone
orientations vary by game and lid. Floors and walls are static bodies, preventing artificial kinematic velocities
when rebuilding lids during size changes. Convex-hull vertices explicitly bake
in the flattened dimensions. Capture
counts, rewind removals and board-size-independent stone dimensions are preserved.

## Selected-move explanation

AI Review now explicitly identifies the move that produced the displayed position:
move number, mover color, coordinate (or Pass), estimated points lost, and the
mover's winning probability before/after, with a signed change in percentage
points. The readout sits at the top of the AI panel. It follows ordinary navigation
as well as mistake arrows; initial positions show no previous move, and missing or
invalid analysis displays Pending instead of a stale value. Consecutive handicap
moves preserve the correct perspective. A self-capture has a textual explanation
and never paints a marker on an empty intersection.

Both renderers force the current red dot during AI Review, without changing saved
marker settings. Move-number overlays yield to the dot so they cannot obscure it.
Hover previews and local scoring suppress this emphasis; leaving AI Review restores
the existing marker preference. Red-arrow neighbor lookup uses the engine's current
index so rapid navigation need not wait for the view's published position update.

probes pass (SelectedMoveFeedback, ResultsMistakesLids, AIVariation). The selected-move
probe checks the actual marked 3D stone at K9 after a White mistake, narrow readout
rendering, unchanged marker preferences, AI/mode gating, and missing-analysis state.
2D/3D marker images and readout screenshots were inspected. Previous app archived in
the dated Selected-move-feedback backup; all prior backups retained. No main-app
preferences or games changed by tests or installation.

## On-stone selected-move feedback

Replaced the AI panel explanation card with two compact labels on the current
stone, preserving the center red dot. The upper label shows estimated point loss
(pt); the lower label shows the signed change in the mover's win probability in
percentage points (pp). For example, 35% to 20% is −15.0pp, not a relative 15%
decrease. Both black and white stones use contrasting outlined text. 3D labels
follow the stone and camera; their annotation material avoids depth clipping
against the curved mesh. AI updates refresh labels without navigating. Missing
analysis leaves the red dot alone. Initial positions, passes, self-captures,
hover previews, scoring and non-review modes never fabricate an on-board label.
The opaque mistake-filter and statistics controls are retained.

## Screensaver integrated into the main app

in the app's gear/settings panel. Users can select a separate SGF folder, enable
Quiet Go playback, refresh, preview, and open macOS Screen Saver settings. Native
selection remains a system setting; the app does not alter wallpaper, lock or idle
policy. Every normal Xcode build packages the signed saver and its preview host.

The app validates and prepares private playback copies, including subfolders,
without the old 80-game cap. It atomically publishes complete generations and
retains the previous one for readers. Failed folder replacement preserves the
existing library. The cache cannot recursively import itself. Copies refresh at
app launch or via Refresh; source changes while the app is closed require the
next refresh. Original SGFs remain untouched. Only the app accesses/bookmarks the
source; the system saver reads the separate Application Support library.

immediate repeats with multiple games, holds the last move for seven seconds and
continues indefinitely. Enablement and new library generations apply to active
playback. Added soft stone shadows with lighting direction matching the main 2D
board, and corrected small-board star points.
