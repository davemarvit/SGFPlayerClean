Local image quality and placement competition — stage 3 pilot (2026-11-16)

Decision: keep all new estimates offline and non-authoritative. Do not promote

The production changes in 0.190 only add explicit correction of a latest saved
false placement. The existing stage-2 recognition/timing decisions remain intact.

What was evaluated
Tools/local_quality_lab.py rectifies captured pixels using each saved homography.
At every intersection it samples surrounding board wood, excluding the potential
stone body at EVERY intersection. Exclusions never depend on ground-truth labels
or classifier confidence. It records local darkening relative to global exposure,
background residual and causal temporal change, sample coverage and a heuristic
quality score. Insufficient coverage is unknown, never evidence of clean wood.
Temporal support requires recorded analyzed frames within 0.5 seconds. Global
exposure is retained separately. Background recovery requires three qualifying
frames spanning 0.4 seconds; actual hand-shadow clearance remains unknown.

Every output preserves the raw readings and available recorded frame decisions,
plus separate counterfactual outputs. No source capture, saved observation, SGF,
recorder decision or clock press is rewritten. Current timestamps are copied, not
reconstructed from the quality scores. Static photos have no temporal history.

Placement competition means alternative locations for ONE physical placement.
The prototype assigns relative ranking mass to expected-color candidates and a
no-move-or-unknown alternative. A strong candidate reduces the relative share of
weak competitors, without deleting their raw evidence. Two equally supported
candidates cannot force a winner. These shares are NOT calibrated probabilities.
Hand blockage, a missing gate, camera gaps, changed retained stones (including
possible captures), or opposite-color additions force abstention. Alternating
turns and capture legality remain responsibilities of the existing recorder.
A single-placement condition is an assumption of this experiment, not a universal
one-stone-per-image rule or a replacement for multi-move gap recovery.

The existing production system already uses alternating colors, legal complete
board positions, captured-stone handling and anchored earlier observations. It
has no general soft normalized competition among all candidate locations. This
experiment measures that additional idea without replacing those constraints.

Data and provenance
- 106 preserved rapid-reply images, frames 305–410. Native pixel-classifier
  readings come from the prior saved baseline; image-quality features are newly
  computed from pixels. Recorded gates/decisions come from the tracked
  RapidReplyShadow sequence fixture; 31 images have no analyzed-frame decision.
- 22 other labeled photo fixtures (shaded, faint, Go club, capture, jade). Five
  incident photos in the old 27-photo corpus are excluded to avoid double-counting.
- 16 timeline frames were visually inspected in rectified contact/keyframe sheets
  and labeled in local_quality_labels.json. They cover before placement and three
  real placements. Uninspected or occluded frames are not scored for accuracy.
- Labels describe physical occupancy, including retained prisoners where the
  earlier fixture labels show them. Episode baselines are explicitly provided
  prior positions, not labels inferred from the current frame.
- This is a development pilot, not an independent held-out evaluation. Consecutive
  frames are correlated. Fixed initial thresholds were used without fitting them
  to outcome counts. A subsequent no-visible-wood guard fixes missing-data
  handling; it does not tune thresholds. Input and program hashes are recorded.

Results (LocalQualityEvaluationResults.json)
Other fixtures: 7,942 intersections across 22 photos. Existing readings have
7,941 correct and one missed stone. Demoting weak stone readings when local
quality is poor introduces 13 uncertain readings without fixing that miss.
Timeline labels: 5,776 intersections across 16 photos. Existing readings have
5,723 correct, four false stones and 49 uncertain readings. The same demotion
rule leaves all four false stones and increases uncertainty to 51.

Conditional placement competition on those 16 timeline views:
- Without quality weighting: seven correct selections, zero wrong, nine abstentions.
- With quality weighting: five correct selections, zero wrong, eleven abstentions.
These are repeated views of three moves, not seven/five independent recognized
moves. Abstention includes the two no-move views and unavailable/blocked gates;
this count is not a comparison with production move or clock accuracy.

Why not promote
Quality weighting suppresses the real Black placement at raw point 309 in frames
355 and 356. Those very observations must survive to prevent the previously
replayed clock pause. Broad background change is not sufficient to reject a
correctly visible stone. The B19 edge region also has insufficient wood coverage
under the frozen sampling rule; its quality stays unknown. In frame 369 the
native false Black at B19 has confidence 1, so a weak-reading-only rule cannot
remove it. A confident isolated phantom requires a no-move model and shape /
background evidence, not merely ranking it against other placements.

Next experiment should distinguish stable stone evidence from changes in nearby
wood, improve edge/background coverage, and test separate no-move evidence. Use
new labeled episodes and the existing negative controls before adjusting any
production thresholds. Do not retroactively discard known-good timing witnesses
merely because a shadow or hand appeared later. Preserve candidate-specific first
appearance, supported placement time and later certification time separately.
