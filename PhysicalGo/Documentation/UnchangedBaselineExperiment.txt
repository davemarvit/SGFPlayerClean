Unchanged-board reference experiment — offline stage 4, 2026-11-16

Decision
Keep the experiment offline. No application, recognition, clock, capture, saved
recording, or live reference code was changed. The guarded reference adaptation
has not demonstrated an accuracy benefit over the same guards with a fixed
reference. Absence of a hand detection is useful context, not proof of no move.

Implementation
Tools/unchanged_baseline_lab.py is a standalone experiment over actual captured
pixels and saved native classifier outputs. Tools/unchanged_baseline_photo_stress.py
adds a clearly synthetic persistence stress using other captured photographs.
Tools/unchanged_baseline_quiet_replay.py processes a longer unchanged interval
from a native diagnostic archive using its stored unadapted readings.

Each experiment starts with an explicitly trusted position and image. The
estimator cannot access subsequent ground-truth labels. It samples the stone
center and nearby wood separately, including one-sided wood samples at board
edges. All possible stone bodies are excluded from the wood samples regardless
of the classifier's predictions. The wood estimates illumination relative to the
original image. The center must still independently resemble the original empty
appearance under that illumination; global darkness is not evidence of a stone
or evidence of emptiness by itself.

Five experimental policies are compared:
- fixed_reference: repeated direct center match to a fixed reference, without
  the additional wood/lighting guard. This is an experimental override policy,
  NOT the production classifier or its existing known-empty lighting behavior.
- no_hand_only: deliberately weak control that assumes a currently clear,
  stable-looking interval is unchanged and can replace empty-patch texture.
- no_interaction_since_seed: stricter control matching the proposed temporal
  assumption. Any detected hand, unknown hand result, camera gap or established
  position contradiction invalidates the interval permanently until a new
  trusted seed is supplied. A wholly missed hand can still fool this policy.
- guarded_fixed: original center texture plus independent wood/illumination
  compatibility; no adaptation. This separates the guard from the benefit of
  adaptation itself.
- guarded_adaptive: the same guards, with versioned illumination updates. It
  keeps original center texture fixed. Candidate learning requires four views
  over 0.6 s plus a 0.8 s quarantine. A newly activated reference requires fresh
  empty witnesses. A subsequent hand/unknown gate or camera gap can roll back
  recently activated references without deleting original observations.

Decisions are computed BEFORE learning from that frame. Unknown coverage remains
unknown. Updates never train on corrected occupancy output. Empty support
requires three observations spanning 0.4 s. An interval over 0.5 s is a camera
gap; timestamps are never compressed to hide missing images. Established stone
contradictions suspend the experiment, including possible capture/removal. Only
independently compatible empty regions can adapt; other points remain frozen.
This is not a legal game resolver, move certifier, capture detector or clock model.

Protocol and provenance
Thresholds were fixed before reading outcome counts; they were not tuned to fix
B19. Initial v1 artifacts remain preserved. v2 adds the stricter no-interaction
control and guarded-fixed ablation; it does not change the guarded estimator's
thresholds. The earlier weak control must not be confused with the stronger
claim that no interaction occurred anywhere in the entire trusted interval.
The quiet wrapper's final rerun corrects seed metadata to 410; the estimator and
thresholds are unchanged. Output manifests hash scripts, source data and images.

Rapid reply
75 analyzed captured frames from the preserved frames 305–410 incident, seeded
at 306 after move 7 (six physical stones; an earlier captured white is absent).
The original capture times and image contents are retained. Model decisions
are compared with the saved native pixel-classifier corpus, not live recorder
state. Fourteen reviewed frames after the seed have both labels and recorded
analysis metadata. The seed is not scored; labeled image 373 has no analyzed-frame
metadata and is excluded. Thus these totals differ from stage 3's 16-photo
snapshot totals; they should not be compared as if their denominators matched.

Eleven scenarios: original delivery; all hands missed; all hand results unknown;
missed hand around each of three placements; a camera gap across each placement;
all three gaps plus missed remaining hands; hand metadata delayed by 0.6 s.
Hand/gap scenarios are injected faults, not claims that the original detector
actually missed all those hands. No image is fabricated. Source frame identity,
original raw readings and capture timestamps remain intact in every scenario.

On the 14 labeled original-delivery frames, native readings contain two false
stones and 47 uncertain readings. Both guarded policies preserve all real stones
but leave those two false readings and 47 uncertainties unchanged. The adaptive
policy actually performs 998 point-reference updates and five rollbacks; the
lack of recognition benefit is not because learning was disabled.

The guarded policies produce no new empty support at a labeled real-stone point
in any of the eleven scenarios. The actual Black timing witnesses at frames
355–356 remain unmodified. The weak current-hand-only control absorbs all three
real placements on labeled later views. The strict no-interaction control does
not harm the scored rapid-reply views, partly because a real 0.576 s delivery
gap at frame 337 invalidates its trusted interval. The photo stress and synthetic
unit case expose its blind spot when no gap or hand is reported at all.

Two remaining rapid-reply false readings are raw point 304 at frame 367 and raw
point 323 (B19 in display rotation 1) at frame 369, both with classifier confidence
1.0. At B19, nearby wood darkens rapidly: estimated gain changes from about 0.74
at frame 354 to 0.83 at 357, and to 0.60 at 369. These are not stable quiet-window
conditions for refreshing a reference. Frame 369 also fails the independent
center-appearance test. Loosening the threshold just to erase this example would
not establish safety on real stones. This is the earlier captured B19 phantom,
not the later saved false move 11 whose exact image was unavailable.

Other captured photographs
22 non-overlapping labeled corpus photos, including faint, shaded, Go club,
capture/prisoner and jade-white fixtures. Each original empty reference is a
trusted seed. The captured test photo is held for a synthetic five seconds with
all hand flags clear. This is an artificial appearance/persistence stress, NOT
an observed video sequence or evidence of real placement timing. Repeated frames
are not counted as independent examples. Some photos contain many stones; this
is not a claim that those stones could all be played between two camera frames.

Count any erroneous empty support during the hold, rather than selecting a
convenient final frame. The fixed-reference override gives false empty support
for 21 stone/photo pairs across 14 photos. Both unguarded no-hand controls absorb
all 271 real stone/photo pairs across 19 nonempty photos. Both guarded policies
produce zero erroneous empty support on those tested photographs. The 271 pairs
are repeated photographed positions, not 271 independent physical placements.
The guards help relative to these unsafe experimental controls; adaptation adds
no demonstrated benefit over guarded_fixed and is not promoted on that basis.

Longer quiet interval
Frames 410–610 of the original diagnostic archive, seeded at the reviewed position
after move 10. Nine evenly spaced contact-sheet images were visually inspected:
410, 435, 460, 485, 510, 535, 560, 585, 610. They show the same nine physical stones,
no visible hand, and empty B19. Only those reviewed frames carry truth labels.
157 analyzed images span 40.006 seconds. Eight reviewed outcome frames (seed
excluded) contain 2,888 intersections, all already correct in recorded unadapted
readings. Every experimental policy preserves those correct outcomes. The guarded
adaptive policy makes 6,336 point-reference updates without changing classifications.
This verifies operation during a longer quiet period, but supplies no evidence
that adaptation fixes genuine long-term lighting drift; no errors occurred in the
reviewed sample. Selected original event rows and analyzed images are preserved
outside the diagnostic rotation pool (hard links/copies, not edited pixels).

What this establishes and what remains open
The implementation can distinguish unavailable hand tracking from a clear view,
preserve real stones in these guarded tests, quarantine/retract reference updates,
and refuse to bridge missing camera time. No-hand-only adaptation has concrete
counterexamples. A fresh baseline is useful only if we first establish that its
appearance really belongs to the unchanged position; it cannot establish its own
correctness by remembering a mistaken classification.

We have not demonstrated improved recognition, fewer live clock pauses, or
calibrated probabilities. Labels are sparse and the replay is one correlated
session. The synthetic photo hold does not replace real missed-hand videos.
Clocks/captures stay under existing live authority; no timing is reconstructed.
The fixed/adaptive guarded tie argues against adding adaptive complexity now.
The next useful test is independent unchanged-position evidence in competition
between no placement and candidate locations, plus genuine long quiet lighting-
drift examples. Preserve frame-level evidence rather than forcing transient
shadows into an updated reference.
