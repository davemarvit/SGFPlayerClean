Connected deferred-location recording
2026-11-17 — stage 16

ARCHITECTURE AND RELEASE SCOPE
GameRecorder still receives native, chronological turn evidence. LocalGameClock
remains the only clock owner. RecordingStoneLedger copies its accepted receipts;
location work cannot call a clock or create a move. Known turns survive exhaustion
of complete candidate positions as ordered records with independent candidate sets.
The existing native legal-position search remains useful when it can resolve
locations, captures or move order. Its failure to choose an intersection does not
by itself require a clock review when independent turn evidence is already valid.

Stable IDs, colors, move order, original press estimates and their clock receipts
are retained in the existing pending sidecar. Corrections update locations without
replaying clock presses. Legacy untimed moves and passes keep unknown timing;
handicap/setup stones are not turns. Undo archives removed identities. Historical
coordinate corrections preserve IDs and measured times in the corrected copy.
Damaged identity data protects the original files from further writes. Native
clock/review persistence remains authoritative; this is not a second clock or a
claim of atomic power-loss durability across all the existing recording files.

CLEANUP AND CONTINUITY
Three distinct clear observations spanning at least 0.8 seconds can retain an
isolated physical-location proposal within 2.25 grid spacings. The historical
intersection stays unresolved. The display uses the physical proposal separately
from the canonical Go board, so the same token is not drawn twice. That proposal
cannot authorize a capture or silently rewrite SGF.

Same-color stones can be accounted for as a group without choosing their individual
identities. A bounded bipartite matching check establishes that all group members
can fit the observed locations; it stores no chosen permutation or history tree.
All members retain the unresolved group. A missing member cannot be explained by
counting the same surviving sighting twice. Independent subsequent turn evidence
can advance play while the assignment remains unresolved.

Hand coverage, unknown hand state, lighting failures and interruptions invalidate
fresh observation runs. Clear absence alone is not a legal capture. Known capture
removal debt remains excluded from new-turn candidates until sustained removal
observations; the existing capture clock policy is unchanged. Unreliable surrounding
stones do not become automatic evidence of cleanup. Timing gaps, contradictory turn
order, uncertain expiry and manual pauses retain the native review requirements.
Confidence thresholds are heuristics, not calibrated probabilities of identity.

REVIEW
Marked locations remain available for review. If complete candidate paths have
been lost, a dedicated review accepts the intersections of the retained ordered
turns, checks the resulting sequence with native Go rules, and finalizes the
existing provisional clock receipts exactly once. Browsing does not pause play;
explicitly applying a correction uses the existing review pause. Stale reviews are
rejected. Existing placement review saves its updated candidate history and stone
location state together in the same pending-sidecar replacement.

VALIDATION SCOPE
The full-game comparison replays original chronological cached observation readings,
outline shapes, hand states and timestamps through the actual GameRecorder with
the feature disabled and enabled. Saved move decisions are evaluation targets only.
A supplied empty starting board is used to initialize both variants; it is not
additional recorded evidence. Camera setup preceding the recorded Start action
is excluded. Each recording retains its own saved camera/grid calibration.

Both variants reproduce the exact 65- and 85-move saved sequences. The 85-move game
retains all 85 measured presses with identical timestamps and no timing review.
The 65-move replay reaches the same first-turn timing review in both variants:
its reprocessed evidence crosses a native timing hold or lacks a retained boundary.
It is therefore NOT counted as 65 independently validated live clock presses.
The native guard is preserved; no saved timestamp is injected to make this test pass.

Large cleanup and ambiguous same-color movement checks use explicitly constructed
observations. The stage-15 translated controls are modified observations derived
from real recordings, not additional real large-cleanup recordings. Existing
recorded repositioning, crowded-board, rapid-reply, hand/clock and capture fixtures
remain separate regressions. Results and source hashes belong to each saved run.

CAMERAS AND UNRELATED WORK
Detector geometry, calibration and the clock engine are unchanged. The AUSDOM AF660
and older zoomed iPhone 11 Pro Max recordings retain their separate calibrations.
The stage-11 grid/lens experiment is not promoted. Original recordings are read-only.
Eleven pre-existing modified tracked files are checked against their initial hashes;
unrelated modified and untracked files are excluded from this change's commits.
The app build uses the existing working tree, including that preserved prior work.

INSTALLATION
The ordinary source build keeps this feature disabled. The release preparation tool
requires a successful validation artifact matching the built executable, enables
PhysicalGoDeferredLocations in the candidate's Info.plist, signs with the stable
existing signing identity, and verifies the signature. Optional installation keeps
a verified previous bundle and replaces the app for its next launch. It never quits
or relaunches the running app and does not modify recording files.

VERIFIED TEST RESULTS
The frozen-source release-validation run passed 56 connected recorder assertions,
all 14 enabled-feature regression suites, all four disabled-feature compatibility
suites, and all 32 Python identity/occlusion tests. Both full recordings reproduced
their complete saved move sequences; the 85-move replay retained identical measured
presses, and the 65-move replay retained the baseline timing-review safeguard
described above. Recorded capture, repositioning and crowded-board cases passed.

The resume fixture without a retained press now tests the actual review workflow:
location confirmation preserves the missing-time warning, the players explicitly
agree clock balances, and fresh board verification resumes the correct player.
The new total of retained turns is not mistaken for the number of known positions
when reviewing an orphaned clock press. No native timing guard was waived.

Earlier attempts and their failures remain in the artifact folder. They exposed
a duplicate display, stale location markers after explicit review, the review
count mismatch, and a post-game marker routing to the missed-move editor. The final
checks cover the fixes. Superseded builds interrupted during editing are not
counted as successful validation. The signed-build result, executable hash and
installation/rollback paths are in validation.json and release/release.json.
