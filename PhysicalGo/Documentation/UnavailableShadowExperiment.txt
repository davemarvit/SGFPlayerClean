Unavailable shadow evidence — offline experiment, 2026-11-16

Result

The selective contrast fallback preserves strong support for all 65 reviewed
placements while withholding support from the reviewed false Black reading at
frame 1281, raw point 75. It also preserves the older photo control's supported
stones and the older replay's decisions and timestamps. This is a promising
development result, not a live release or an independent validation result.

No live recognition, clock, capture, application settings, or native Swift
sources were changed by this task. Unrelated scoring edits in the shared
workspace were left untouched. The only existing implementation modified is
the OFFLINE PlacementCompetition class: an optional filter can reduce support,
but cannot increase it, discard raw readings, infer emptiness, or bypass guards.

What was tested

All policies retain the existing Black-specific shadow weighting whenever
appearance is available. Missing appearance is evaluated separately:

neutral: the existing fallback keeps the raw support.
hold_all: hold any candidate whose appearance is unavailable.
hold_black: apply that hold to Black only, preserving the White control.
outline_black: permit missing Black appearance with an existing current-frame
  stone-outline fit of quality >=0.90 and center offset <=0.20 per axis.
contrast_black: permit missing Black appearance only when background mismatch
  is the sole failure and measured center darkening exceeds the surrounding
  wood residual by >=0.14. All other missing/invalid evidence stays pending.

The contrast measurement is median(current core - gain * reference core).
Gain must remain within the existing [0.4, 1.8] bounds; at least 90% of the
core must be visible, enough wood samples must exist, and the core/background
metrics must be finite. The original signedCoreDelta remains unavailable.
The extra measurement does not silently turn the failed background check into
a successful one. The 0.14 margin reuses the existing full-contrast threshold;
no parameter search was performed.

This contrast margin is a heuristic, NOT a probability or statistical error
bound. In particular, a localized hand shadow can still imitate a stone, so
hand, exposure, temporal, legality, and capture checks remain authoritative.
Available appearance that contradicts a stone cannot be overruled by either
fallback. White behavior is unchanged in the selective policies.

Development history is retained: v1's outline-only rule failed at Black 21
(outline quality 0.8755, below 0.90). Its threshold was not relaxed. v2 adds
the separate contrast ablation. Both the earlier game and this 65-move game
have now influenced development; neither is a clean holdout.

The older photos contain 247 correct stone samples above the single-frame
support threshold in the neutral control. contrast_black retains all 247;
hold_black loses 20, and hold_all loses 30. Photo outline metadata is absent,
so outline_black is conservatively held when that evidence is unavailable.

Complete one-stone-missing photo probes are also checked separately: a known
prior position is supplied, the photo is repeated 25 times, and the original
full-board decision guards remain active. Those synthetic repetitions are not
independent images or real played moves. The verifier compares every decision
and timing field against the neutral control and the stage6 aggregate.
It retains 230 of 271 probes with zero wrong locations and identical decisions
and timing fields across all 6,775 paired synthetic frames.

Temporal evidence and safeguards

The focused video includes 95 captured point observations. Visual review labels
78 and excludes 17 occluded/ambiguous views. The contrast fallback retains all
16 strongly supported correct stone observations, including all nine in frames
whose recorded hand/exposure gates are clear. It removes support from two
false-stone observations at the same empty edge, including one clear-gated view.
These are two readings in one event, not two independent failure cases.

The original focused windows encounter >0.5-second analysis gaps. Every policy
leaves review latched; no policy declares a placement across those gaps. A
separate, explicitly conditional probe supplies a reviewed empty point at
frame 534 after the earlier gap. It is not automatic recovery.

In that supplied context, neutral and contrast_black both first see Black 21
at frame 545 (hand blocked), first support it at 547, and obtain their second
witness at 548. They retain exactly the same original capture times. Frame 545
cannot lend its timestamp to the usable 547/548 support run. Subsequent gaps
remain latched, and the result still has no clock authority.

The older rapid-reply replay covers 12 delivery/fault scenarios and four
conditional positions. All 1,300 neutral output rows match the archived stage6
control exactly. contrast_black changes no decision or timing field in those
1,300 rows. Real Black witnesses 355 and 356 remain at their original times,
with confirmation at 356. Removing those witnesses still prevents selection.
Capture/removal, opposite-color, hand, unknown-hand, exposure and gap conditions
cannot be overridden by the filter. Pending evidence never becomes no-move.

An existing limitation remains: injected missed-hand scenarios produce 16
repeated selections using witnesses originally marked hand-blocked, unchanged
from the neutral control. They are not 16 independent events and are not proof
of valid clock times. This experiment does not solve missed-hand detection or
establish native clock accuracy. No live timing behavior was modified.
