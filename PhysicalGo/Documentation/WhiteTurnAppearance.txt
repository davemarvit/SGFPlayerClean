CHANGE
With deferred locations enabled, independent white-disk evidence may support a
turn when the point score is at least 0.80. It requires a measured footprint with
quality at least 0.98, plausible radius/aspect ratio, a nearby assigned point,
explicit hand clearance and unblocked lighting. Placement fits alone do not count.
One outline cannot establish two stones or borrow a neighboring stone's outline.
At least six supported observations must span a full second, with no gap greater
than 0.65 seconds and centers remaining within 0.12 grid spacings. Faster camera
delivery increases the number of observations required to cover that second.
The existing surrounding-position consistency check still applies.
If subsequent observations satisfy the ordinary high-score policy, that policy
can confirm the turn even if outline support becomes unavailable. An early weaker
point reading cannot veto those later strong observations or change the original
supported placement time.

The accepted turn retains the FIRST supported placement estimate. Neither the
raw reading score nor the exact-position warning is rewritten. The stone ledger
also uses these outlines to account for existing white stones; its sustained
cleanup checks, unresolved identities and legal capture handling remain in force.
The black/shadow policy, camera calibration, detector and clock engine are unchanged.
Both AUSDOM AF660 and older zoomed iPhone 11 Pro Max calibration remain separate.
