Pre-hand shadow experiments and bounded follow-ups
2026-11-16 — OFFLINE ONLY, no policy promoted

Decision

Keep outline-aware background sampling as the leading candidate. It restores
the real Black 13 evidence without losing any reviewed placement or previously
selected move in these comparisons. Do not promote a hard outline requirement,
or interpret a later hand as proof that earlier dark readings were shadows.
Neither retrospective discounting nor extra edge persistence has demonstrated
additional false-move prevention over the existing temporal gates.

The current app, Start behavior, clock accounting and capture resolver are
unchanged. Both recent games are development evidence, not untouched validation.

Experiments

Six fixed policies were crossed with original and outline-masked pixel features:
control; retrospectively discount pending Black evidence; restrict that discount
to the outer edge; additionally require missing eligible outline; immediately
withhold outline-less Black edge evidence; combine the last two policies.

The lookback is the existing .8 seconds, with no threshold search. Revisions
become available only when the later hand observation arrives. Capture time,
delivery time, original reading, original weight and prior decisions remain
separate. Already emitted placements cannot be retracted. A camera gap breaks
the retrospective link and retains the original review latch. Suppression never
means an intersection is empty. These tools never calculate a clock press.

Full recordings supply 1,385 and 1,658 analyzed frames. Pixel features were
cached sparsely for every candidate relevant to the supplied test windows;
unmeasured empty-point appearance stays unknown. There are 150 conditional turn
contexts and four additional reviewed edge-error contexts. Original observations
are retained, including hand-obstructed frames. Saved chronology supplies the
prior board and turn; this is not an autonomous full-game decoder.

Faults: recorded signals, suppressed hand-state detections, unknown hand state,
600ms-delayed hand state, omitted 900ms camera intervals, gaps plus missed hands,
and unavailable outline evidence. Outline dropout with original pixels tests
complete outline loss; the masked-pixel/dropout arm isolates loss at the decision
gate while retaining the cached feature. Faults change supplied signals, not the
original native detector or its already-produced raw readings.

Older positive and failure coverage

Twenty-two older photos supply 271 synthetic missing-one-stone placement probes.
Original and masked sampling both select 230, with no wrong location and no
regression. The corpus contains 12 Black edge point/frame examples, representing
only TWO DISTINCT STONES in ONE setup across six frames. All 12 have eligible
outlines; ten probes are selectable under the existing board guards.

The hard outline gate removes the five strong false edge readings. With recorded
outlines it retains 230/271 older probes, but with all outlines unavailable it
drops to 220/271: all ten previously selectable Black edge probes are lost.
Absent geometry can mean unavailable color correction, rejected/conflicting fits
or occlusion; it does not establish absence of a stone. Do not promote this gate.

The resulting follow-up replaces permanent rejection with .8 seconds of sustained
current-run evidence for outline-less Black edge candidates. It recovers all
230/271 synthetic probes, adding .6 seconds to ten of them. It changes zero of
15,100 recent-game control decision rows across feature/fault combinations.
There is no demonstrated extra false-decision benefit. A sustained shadow with a
missed hand can still pass; a dedicated counterexample test records that limit.
Synthetic repeated-photo delay is not measured game latency.

Older real rapid-play replay covers 48 scenario/context combinations and 1,300
decision rows per feature/policy, including missed-hand, camera-gap and missing
Black timing-witness cases. All six policies and both feature variants preserve
the control decisions and observation timing. There are no wrong selected points
or guard violations. Sixteen selections involving originally hand-blocked
witnesses remain under fault injection: an existing weakness, not a safety pass.

Recent-game missed-hand injections likewise expose the unchanged weakness:
38 selection rows in the 65-game and 67 in the masked 85-game occur on originally
obstructed frames. These are repeated decision rows, not 105 distinct moves.
The experiment does not infer absence of a hand from a missing signal.

Next evidence needed

More threshold tuning on these same games would not establish generalization.
The useful next collection is an untouched normal game under a different light
or camera angle, with some Black edge placements and ordinary captures. No exact
sequence is required. Retain empty edge periods with hands near the board too.
Until that exists, keep all new recognition rules offline. In particular, do
not trade capture/timing guards for a higher conditional recognition count.
