Clock behavior

Start still starts the initial player's countdown: Black in an even game,
White after handicap setup verification. Nothing makes the first move free.

LocalGameClock now retains the current turn's starting balances and automatic
hold intervals. When a verified press arrives late, accounting replays the
running segments and excludes every stopped interval. This works for individual
moves, multi-move catch-up and deferred live presses. The original press time
remains attached to the move; increments and overtime resets occur once.

An estimate inside a hold, overlapping its boundary, preceding the retained
turn, or lying in the future cannot silently fall back to recognition time.
Confirmed moves with such timing require explicit clock review. Candidate-only
timestamps cannot backdate across a hold. Unsupported provisional candidates
stay pending; they neither advance saved clock history nor prematurely require
clock review. Their camera observations remain in the recorder.

A press exactly at the hold's start is applied before suspension. Its running
anchor is retained in the move record, and the clock remains stopped afterward.
A press exactly at resume excludes all preceding hold time. Regression checks
cover both boundary cases.

Manual pauses, agreed clock corrections, undo, restore, and capture transitions
remain separate boundaries. Manual pause/review discards retrospective authority.
Automatic capture removal retains its existing free-removal-time policy, minimum
capture threshold, completion timestamp and one-time increment/reset. A camera
interruption during removal does not restart a clock or charge removal time.

The Nov 16 startup incident

StartupClockReplay.swift uses the original uptime values from the 85-move game.
The protective hold still occurs: the freshness threshold and Start behavior
were not relaxed. The previously silent allocation is now prevented. The first
three estimates are preserved as unapplied, neither clock runs, and clock review
is required. The old saved game is not rewritten, and 120/120 is a held display
pending review, not a claim that no thinking time elapsed.

This repairs the unsafe accounting path; it does not remove the startup callback
stall that triggered the hold. That performance issue still needs profiling.
Known presses wholly before a hold can now be recovered automatically. Moves
inside an unresolved held interval need agreed times rather than invented ones.
