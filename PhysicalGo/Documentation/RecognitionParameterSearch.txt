Offline recognition parameter search — 2026-11-16

First run
53 configurations: incumbent, 18 one-axis endpoints, 24 random combinations,
and 10 remaining unique neighboring refinements. Forty-five passed the training
safety checks; eight produced empty evidence against real stones and were
rejected. Every trial left the same two phantom point/case pairs and 47 uncertain
point/frame readings. The incumbent therefore won the tie. There is no measured
recognition improvement and no reason to change live settings.

Both the incumbent and selected result (the same settings) preserved all labeled
real stones in the reserved photo groups and all ten additional hand/gap fault
scenarios. The reserved photos retain one existing classifier miss; this stage
neither fixes nor hides it. Twenty phantom point/scenario pairs in fault checks
are the same two underlying false readings repeated across ten scenarios, not
twenty independent incidents.

Sensitivity is informative: coreError=0.09 suppressed six real stone/photo pairs,
and woodError=0.07 suppressed four. Neither removed the phantom readings. At
those readings (367/raw304 and 369/raw323), direct core errors against the
unadapted seed were approximately 0.145 and 0.252. No reference adaptation had
activated at those points; the hand gate had only recently cleared. This is
consistent with insufficient stable evidence for this baseline strategy, not
an optimizer failing to select the most permissive settings. It does not prove
that another representation or candidate-selection stage cannot help.

All 19 offline tests passed. The scalar and vectorized traces agree. A further
comparison against preserved stage-4 output matched all 1,478 decisions across
34 cases, including adaptations, rollbacks and original timestamps. Live Swift
fingerprints and the captured observations are unchanged. Native clock/capture
suites were not rerun because no native code or integration changed.

See RecognitionParameterSearchResults.json for the compact result snapshot and
the external artifact folder's plan, manifests, trial records and full traces.

Threshold search alone has not shown a benefit on the errors in this corpus.

Scope
This first search tunes the guarded unchanged-board evidence experiment. It
does not tune the entire recognizer. No application setting, Swift source, SGF,
clock balance, capture archive, or installed app is written by the search.
The result remains experimental even if training and validation improve.

Tools/recognition_parameter_space.json is the reproducible search specification.
Its nine axes are:
- coreError: maximum difference from the original empty intersection texture.
- woodError: maximum residual in surrounding wood after estimating lighting.
- minimumGain, maximumGain: accepted illumination scale relative to the seed.
- stableGainRange: variation allowed while collecting adaptation witnesses.
- learningFrames, learningSeconds: evidence required for a lighting adaptation.
- emptyWitnesses, emptySeconds: evidence required to support an empty point.

These cover appearance compatibility, illumination adaptation and temporal
empty evidence. Native stone shape/color scoring, hand/shadow detection,
single-placement competition, move certification and confidence calibration are
not optimized here. The earlier competition prototype has separate knobs, but
it is not yet part of this search. Clock/capture rules are constraints, not axes.

Safeguards
Every estimator receives an isolated read-only configuration. Only the nine
named axes can be overridden; trying to change any other protocol key fails.
The 0.5 s continuity limit, 0.8 s quarantine/rollback window, sampling geometry,
minimum coverage and minimum wood sample count remain fixed. Unknown/detected
hands, exposure failures and gaps cannot establish an unchanged interval.
An established stone disappearing or becoming uncertain blocks evidence,
including when a legitimate capture could be in progress. This stage does not
resolve captures or infer legal moves; the legal resolver remains separate.

The learner can adjust illumination only. It cannot replace the original empty
texture with an arriving stone. Decisions precede learning on each frame. Late
hand signals can revoke recent adaptations without rewriting observations.
Real-stone empty support is a rejection even when the raw classifier already
missed that stone. Thus a pre-existing miss cannot hide an unsafe override.
Black's actual rapid-reply witnesses at frames 355/356, raw point 309, must not
receive empty support. Original raw readings, captured times and pixel patches
are fingerprinted before and after evaluation. Live Swift files are also hashed.

Preserving those two witnesses is not a new measurement of clock accuracy.
This experiment does not produce presses, recover elapsed time, or exercise
the native legal/capture resolver. Integration would require the existing
native replay benchmark, all clock configurations and capture regressions.

Data and split
The recorded rapid-reply interval and longer quiet continuation belong to the
same session. BOTH are training data; adjacent frames are never divided into
training and validation. Their observations retain original capture timestamps.
Rapid reply uses previously rerun native pixel classifications. Quiet replay
uses preserved native unadapted classifications; these sources are identified
separately and are not assumed interchangeable.

Twelve captured photos from shaded, faint and club groups (including their
empty references) are training stress cases. Ten photos from the complete
FaintCaptureQ3 and JadeWhiteA19 groups are reserved from parameter selection.
The five incident photos in the static corpus are excluded because they overlap
the rapid timeline. No group is split between training and validation.

Photo persistence is a synthetic five-second hold, NOT video or real timing.
Each photograph is scored once at the final synthetic frame. Every intermediate
real-stone suppression still rejects the setting. Physical stones awaiting
capture removal retain the physical-occupancy labels from the existing corpus.
The actual timeline is scored only at previously reviewed frames; all other
observations provide causal evidence without being treated as ground truth.

All of these datasets were examined in earlier experiments. Reserved groups
provide development regression checks, not an untouched independent test set.
A fresh camera/board/lighting session is required before a promotion claim.

Search and objective
Run the incumbent, each axis's two endpoints with all others fixed, and 24
seeded random combinations. Refine at up to 12 neighboring grid settings around
the best TRAINING result. This is a bounded search, not a guarantee of a global
optimum. Equal outcomes prefer the smallest normalized departure from existing
settings, so a flat landscape does not produce a spurious new recommendation.

Rank safety first. Then minimize distinct false-stone point/case pairs, then
uncertain point/frame readings. Most intersections are empty, so overall board
accuracy and adaptation counts receive no reward. These measures describe
occupancy evidence; they are not measured phantom move events, paused seconds,
or confirmation latency. Raw missed/wrong-color/uncertain counts are retained
for comparison, and repeated readings are not independent placements.

The selected setting is written to selected.json BEFORE validation is evaluated.
Validation cannot select another winner or drive refinement. The incumbent and
selected setting are checked against both reserved groups and all ten additional
missed-hand, unknown-hand, delayed-hand and camera-gap scenarios. A failed check
rejects the candidate; it must not start an unreported search on validation.
No result is ever promoted automatically.

Reproduction (from the repository root; output must be NEW and external)
python3 PhysicalGo/Tools/recognition_parameter_search.py \
  --corpus '/Users/dave/Documents/SGF PLayer/work/recognizer-comparison' \
  --sequence PhysicalGo/Tests/Fixtures/RapidReplyShadow/sequence.json \
  --quiet '/Users/dave/Documents/SGF PLayer/work/unchanged-baseline-stage4/quiet-replay-final' \
  --workers 3 \
  --output '/absolute/new/report/folder'

Dependencies are the existing Python environment's numpy and opencv-python.
The quiet input is the preserved prior replay folder, not a rotating live archive.
Read/rectify/sample captured images once, then reuse immutable observations.
Worker processes receive only training cases. An optional vectorized residual
calculation is checked against the scalar evaluator, including complete output
traces for the incident and a reserved captured photo at both configurations.
The original experiment remains scalar by default and retains its old settings.

Artifacts
plan.json: search space, fixed protocol, source fingerprint, written before search.
manifest.json: exact data split, input/image/script hashes and runtime versions.
observations.jsonl: original classifier readings, times and measured features.
trials.jsonl: every setting, training outcome and duration, flushed as it finishes.
selected.json: frozen training choice, written before validation.
incumbent/ and selected/: complete per-frame decisions for every evaluated case.
*-scalar-check/: reference evaluator traces for the equivalence checks.
summary.json: outcome, safety/immutability checks and explicit limitations.

Tests
python3 -m unittest discover -s PhysicalGo/Tools -p 'test_*.py' -v
The suite covers configuration isolation, rejected safeguard overrides, scalar
equivalence, all sampled settings' hand/gap/capture gates, unsafe real-stone
absorption controls, late-hand rollback, deterministic selection, incumbent tie
preference, validation exclusion and group split validation, plus prior labs.
